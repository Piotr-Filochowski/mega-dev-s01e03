Not one issue. Three, with three fixes. Fixing the parser alone closes only the largest part.

The problems

  1. Parser reads dates month first. Day-first tenants see day and month swapped, in every channel. This is the bug #4471 describes.
  2. Booking-v3 form hint. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. People in day-first countries type what the hint says, and the form reads it their way. Form channel only. Intake tagged many of these parser, because tenant and form are not part of the tag.
  3. Dates move by one day. Nothing is swapped. The reports talk about one day off, only June, an invite before the booking, and one meeting on two days for two time zones. This points to a time-zone or daylight-saving bug.

Why one ticket was wrong

Decision 0001 merges on subject alone, so reports with the same subject and different causes land together. Counts here come from the report data, tenant locale and booking form, not from subject or intake tag.

Lessons

  • A big ticket can hide several bugs. Check the cause before you merge.
  • Count each problem once, with the reports that belong to it.
